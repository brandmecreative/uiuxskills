What is a digital business card?

A web page with your contact details, social links and work, shared with one link. People open it in their browser by tapping your link, scanning your QR code or tapping an NFC card — no app needed.

Can I add a QR code to my printed card?

Yes. Add a QR code that points to your digital card, so the printed card carries your full, always-up-to-date profile.

Which printed finish should I choose?

Matte for a calm, premium feel; gloss for bright colours and photos; synthetic when cards need to survive water and heavy handling.
