Help

Quick answers

What is a digital card?

A mobile-friendly web page with your photo, contact details and social links. It has its own link and QR code, so anyone can open it on their phone without an app.

How fast do I get it?

Instantly. Your link and QR code appear right after payment.

Can I edit it later?

Yes, any time from My Account. Your link and QR code stay the same.

How long is it valid?

It's billed yearly. Renewal is ₹299 a year, starting a year after you buy. We email you before it's due.

I don't have a design for printed cards

Pick a ready-made design and edit it online, or upload your own JPG or PNG (ideally 300 DPI).

How are printed cards shipped?

By India Post Speed Post with tracking, within India.

How can I pay?

UPI, debit and credit cards, net banking and wallets through PayU. No cash on delivery.
