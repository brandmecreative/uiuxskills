What is a Vueme digital business card?

A mobile-friendly web page with your photo, contact details and social links. It has its own link and QR code, so anyone can open it on their phone without an app.

How quickly do I get my digital card?

Instantly. Your card link and QR code appear right after successful payment, and you can edit the card any time from My Account.

What does the Analytics add-on show?

Views, QR code scans, link taps, call taps and saved contacts, in a private dashboard only you can see. It switches on automatically after payment.

How long is the digital card valid?

The card is billed yearly. Renew before it expires to keep it live; if not renewed, the card becomes inactive until you renew.

Which paper types do you offer for printed cards?

Matte, Gloss and Synthetic, in single side or front and back. Rounded corners are available on Matte and Gloss for ₹100.

How are printed cards shipped?

By India Post Speed Post with tracking. We add the tracking number to your order and email it to you once shipped. We deliver within India only.

Which payment methods do you accept?

UPI, debit and credit cards, net banking and wallets through PayU. Cash on delivery is not available.
